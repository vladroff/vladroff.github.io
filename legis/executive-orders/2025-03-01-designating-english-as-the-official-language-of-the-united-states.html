Designating English as the Official Language of The United States ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Saturday, March 1, 2025

Summary of Executive Order on English as the Official Language ​

Purpose and Policy ​

  • English has historically been the national language of the United States.
  • Foundational documents, including the Declaration of Independence and the Constitution, were written in English.
  • Declaring English as the official language promotes unity, cultural cohesion, and efficient governance.
  • Encouraging new Americans to learn English fosters economic opportunities, community engagement, and civic participation.
  • Establishing a single official language streamlines communication and reinforces shared national values.

Definitions ​

  • Agency: As defined in 44 U.S.C. § 3502, excluding the Executive Office of the President.
  • Agency Head: The highest-ranking official of an agency (e.g., Secretary, Administrator, Chairman, or Director).

Designation of English as the Official Language ​

  • English is officially designated as the sole language of the United States.
  • Revokes Executive Order 13166 (Improving Access to Services for Persons with Limited English Proficiency).
  • Agencies are not required to eliminate multilingual services but may decide how best to fulfill their mission.
  • The Attorney General must rescind guidance issued under Executive Order 13166 and provide updates consistent with the law.

General Provisions ​

  • This order does not override existing legal authorities of agencies.
  • Implementation must comply with applicable laws and budget availability.
  • The order does not create any enforceable legal rights for individuals.

Issued: March 1, 2025 – The White House
