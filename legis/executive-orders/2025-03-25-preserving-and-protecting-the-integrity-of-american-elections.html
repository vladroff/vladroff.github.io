PRESERVING AND PROTECTING THE INTEGRITY OF AMERICAN ELECTIONS ​

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/preserving-and-protecting-the-integrity-of-american-elections/

Published: 2025-03-25T16:17:34-04:00

  • Election Protection Enforcement

    • The U.S. lacks essential election protections compared to other countries.
    • Emphasizes the need for free, fair, and honest elections vital for democracy.
    • States must align with federal laws to ensure election integrity.

  • Voter Eligibility

    • Requires proof of U.S. citizenship on voter registration forms.
    • Provides guidelines for States to verify voter citizenship using federal databases.
    • Demands the Attorney General to prioritize enforcement against non-citizen voting.

  • Voting Equipment and Integrity

    • Promotes uniform voting standards, including a voter-verifiable paper record.
    • Mandates an audit of voting system certifications.
    • States failing to comply with federal guidelines may face funding consequences.

  • Federal Compliance and Enforcement

    • Emphasizes the need for states to adhere to federal laws regarding voting and election day procedures.
    • Outlines potential penalties for states not cooperating with federal election integrity measures.

  • Protection Against Foreign Influence

    • Reinforces prohibitions on foreign participation and funding in elections.
    • Calls for enforcement of laws against foreign donations and lobbying in U.S. elections.

  • Overall Compliance

    • Requires agencies to submit compliance reports.
    • Ensures that any invalid provisions of the order do not affect the remainder.
    • Clarifies that the order does not create enforceable rights against the U.S. government.
