PROMOTING FAIR COMPETITION IN LIVESTOCK MARKETS AND EXPANDING MARKET ACCESS FOR AMERICAN MEAT PRODUCERS ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Friday, September 4, 2026

Headline: Order boosts support for ranchers, small meat processors, and fair meat market competition.
Why it matters: The order tackles monopolistic practices in meat processing, strengthens enforcement of fair competition laws, and expands interstate sales opportunities for local meat producers. It aims to make it easier and fairer for ranchers to process and sell their products nationwide while maintaining top food safety.
What the order does:

  • Increases enforcement of the Packers and Stockyards Act against unfair, deceptive, or anti-competitive conduct by meat processors.
  • Allocates more resources and staff to USDA enforcement divisions.
  • Directs close collaboration between USDA and the Department of Justice on antitrust action.
  • Requires reports on enforcement and market access barriers within 60 days.
  • Streamlines and expands State and Federal meat inspection programs for wider interstate product shipment.
  • Provides technical assistance and training for small meat processors.
  • Creates an accessible web resource with information on local processing facilities.
  • Modernizes inspection requirements and reduces unnecessary federal reporting.
  • Launches a guaranteed loan program for small and regional beef processors. Who it affects:
  • American ranchers and livestock producers.
  • Small and regional meat processors.
  • Large meat processing companies.
  • State and Federal food safety inspectors.
  • Consumers seeking locally produced meat. Practical impact on a regular person:
  • Makes it easier to buy local, safely processed meat from ranchers in other states.
  • May lower meat prices and increase variety due to more competition. Context: Part of a wider federal push to curb market consolidation and promote fair agricultural competition.
