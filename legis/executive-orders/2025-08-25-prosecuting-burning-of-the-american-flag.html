PROSECUTING BURNING OF THE AMERICAN FLAG ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Monday, August 25, 2025

  • The American Flag is a vital symbol of U.S. identity, freedom, and strength, deserving of respect and unity among all Americans.

  • Desecrating the Flag is viewed as an act of contempt and can incite violence and hostility towards the nation.

  • The Supreme Court has not protected flag desecration that incites imminent lawless actions or constitutes “fighting words.”

  • The Administration will work to prosecute individuals who desecrate the Flag in a manner that violates laws and incites violence.

  • Attorney General Actions:

    • Prioritize enforcement against flag desecration under applicable criminal and civil laws.
    • Focus on related violent crimes, hate crimes, discrimination, and property violations.
    • Refer violations of state or local laws to appropriate authorities.

  • Prosecution Guidelines:

    • Prosecute individuals involved in flag desecration that violates laws to the fullest extent permissible.
    • Pursue litigation to define exceptions to First Amendment protections regarding flag desecration.

  • Immigration Measures:

    • Foreign nationals involved in flag desecration may face visa denials, terminations, or deportations under federal law.

  • General Provisions:

    • If any part of the order is invalidated, the remaining provisions will still apply.
    • The order does not affect the authority of executive departments or create enforceable rights against the U.S. or its entities.
    • Implementation must align with applicable laws and budget appropriations.

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/08/prosecuting-burning-of-the-american-flag/
