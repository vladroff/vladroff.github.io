INTEGRATING FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGY INNOVATION INTO REGULATORY FRAMEWORKS ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Tuesday, May 19, 2026

Headline: Executive order pushes for streamlined fintech regulations and expanded access to Federal Reserve payment services.

Why it matters: The order aims to promote financial innovation and competition by modernizing regulations, reducing barriers for fintech firms, and potentially allowing non-banks greater access to payment systems historically controlled by traditional banks.

What the order does:

  • Directs federal financial regulators to review and update rules that impede fintech innovation or partnership with federally regulated institutions.
  • Requires identification and revision of regulations that unnecessarily hinder fintech entry, especially for small and emerging firms.
  • Orders streamlining of application processes for bank and credit union charters, insurance, and related federal licenses for eligible fintech firms.
  • Requests the Federal Reserve to assess and report on legal and regulatory options for granting non-banks and digital asset firms access to Reserve Bank payment services.
  • Encourages transparent, timely application procedures for such access if legally permissible.

Who it affects:

  • Fintech firms, especially startups and small or emerging companies.
  • Federally regulated banks and credit unions.
  • Non-bank financial companies seeking greater market access.
  • Federal financial regulators and the Federal Reserve System.

Practical impact on a regular person:

  • Increased access to innovative financial products and services.
  • Potential for faster, more affordable payment options and improved competition in financial services.

Context: The order explicitly seeks a balance between promoting innovation, ensuring financial stability, and protecting consumers.
