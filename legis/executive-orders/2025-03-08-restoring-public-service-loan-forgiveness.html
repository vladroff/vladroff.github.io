Restoring Public Service Loan Forgiveness ​

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/restoring-public-service-loan-forgiveness/

Published: Saturday, March 8, 2025

Summary of Executive Order on Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF) Program ​

  • Authority: The President enacts this order under the Constitution and U.S. law.

  • Background:

    • PSLF Program was established in 2007 by Congress to incentivize public service jobs.
    • The program promises student loan forgiveness after 10 years of service and minimum payments.

  • Issues Identified:

    • The previous administration abused the PSLF through a waiver process, misallocating taxpayer funds.
    • PSLF misdirects resources into activist organizations that undermine national security and American values.
    • The program potentially increases tuition costs and burdens students with unsuitable debt.

  • National Security:

    • The President’s duty includes ending subsidies for illegal activities that threaten U.S. security, such as:
      • Illegal immigration
      • Human smuggling
      • Child trafficking
      • Public property damage
      • Disruption of public order

  • Policy Changes:

    • Individuals employed by organizations with substantial illegal activities shall be ineligible for PSLF.

  • Definition Revisions:

    • The Secretary of Education will propose changes to 34 C.F.R. 685.219 to redefine “public service” to exclude:
      • Activities violating federal immigration laws.
      • Supporting terrorism or facilitating operations of designated cartels.
      • Child abuse or trafficking children to evade lawful parents.
      • Aiding and abetting illegal discrimination.
      • Violating state tort laws including trespassing, vandalism, and disorderly conduct.

  • Legal Provisions:

    • The order does not impair the authority of executive departments or the Office of Management and Budget.
    • It is subject to applicable laws and budget appropriations.
    • The order does not create enforceable rights against the U.S. or its entities.
