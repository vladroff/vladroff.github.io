MODIFYING THE SCOPE OF THE RECIPROCAL TARIFF WITH RESPECT TO CERTAIN AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Friday, November 14, 2025

Headline: U.S. exempts certain agricultural imports from reciprocal tariffs.

Why it matters: This change removes tariffs from specific agricultural goods, aiming to address national security concerns while supporting domestic demand and production. It directly affects trade flows and prices for key U.S. imports.

What the order does:

  • Removes certain agricultural products from the list of goods subject to reciprocal tariffs.
  • Updates tariff schedules and makes changes effective November 13, 2025.
  • Allows refunds of previously collected duties on newly exempted goods.
  • Directs ongoing monitoring and potential future adjustments by trade and commerce officials.
  • Authorizes agencies to issue rules and take actions needed to implement these changes.

Who it affects:

  • Importers of newly exempted agricultural products.
  • U.S. consumers of those agricultural goods.
  • Domestic agricultural producers.
  • U.S. Customs and Border Protection and related federal agencies.

Practical impact on a regular person:

  • Potentially lower prices or greater availability for affected agricultural products.
  • Faster customs processing and possible refunds for importers of the exempted goods.

Context: This order updates prior tariff measures enacted to address the U.S. goods trade deficit and national security concerns.
