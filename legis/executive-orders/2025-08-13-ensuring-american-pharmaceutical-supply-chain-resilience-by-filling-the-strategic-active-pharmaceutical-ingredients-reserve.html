ENSURING AMERICAN PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY CHAIN RESILIENCE BY FILLING THE STRATEGIC ACTIVE PHARMACEUTICAL INGREDIENTS RESERVE ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Wednesday, August 13, 2025

  • The President orders actions to enhance domestic production of essential pharmaceutical products, referencing Executive Order 13944 from August 2020.

  • Agencies are directed to:

    • Increase domestic procurement of essential medicines and identify supply chain vulnerabilities.
    • The FDA published a list of essential medicines, later refined to 86 by the ASPR.

  • Current statistics:

    • Nearly 40% of finished prescription drugs made in the U.S.
    • Only about 10% of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) are domestically produced.

  • Creation of a Strategic Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients Reserve (SAPIR) to stockpile APIs, aiming to:

    • Insulate from foreign supply constraints, especially from adversary nations.
    • Encourage domestic production through government purchases.

  • Criticism of the current Administration for not advancing domestic sources for essential medicines despite significant spending.

Directives: ​

  1. Within 30 days:

    • ASPR to develop a list of 26 critical drugs and assess available funds for SAPIR.
    • OMB to assist in fund repurposing.

  2. Within 120 days:

    • ASPR to prepare the existing SAPIR repository to maintain APIs.

  3. 6-month supply directive:

    • ASPR to obtain a 6-month supply of APIs for critical drugs with a preference for domestic sources.

  4. Within 90 days:

    • ASPR to update the essential medicines list and present a plan to:
      • Obtain and maintain a 6-month supply of APIs.
      • Propose costs for a second SAPIR repository within one year.

Other Notes: ​

  • The order does not impact existing legal authorities or budgetary functions.
  • Implementations must comply with applicable laws and appropriations.
  • The order does not create enforceable rights against the U.S. or its agencies.

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/08/ensuring-american-pharmaceutical-supply-chain-resilience-by-filling-the-strategic-active-pharmaceutical-ingredients-reserve/
