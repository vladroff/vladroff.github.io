ADDRESSING RISKS FROM WILMERHALE ​

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/addressing-risks-from-wilmerhale/

Published: 2025-03-27T18:34:15-04:00

  • The President has issued an executive order to address risks posed by law firms, particularly "Big Law" firms, that act against American interests.
  • It highlights concerns about law firms engaging in activities that threaten public safety, national security, and constitutional freedoms.
  • The Executive Order specifically targets Wilmer Cutler Pickering Hale and Dorr LLP (WilmerHale) for alleged abuses of its pro bono practice and partisan conduct.
  • Accusations against WilmerHale include:
    • Discrimination based on race
    • Obstruction of illegal immigration prevention measures
    • Supporting noncitizen voting
    • Weaponizing the justice system through influential hires like Robert Mueller.
  • The order mandates the following:
    • Suspension of security clearances for WilmerHale employees until a review is complete.
    • The Office of Management and Budget must identify and cease government support and contracts with WilmerHale.
    • Federal contractors must disclose any business dealings with WilmerHale related to government contracts.
    • Agencies must review contracts with WilmerHale and take steps, including possible terminations, to align with national interests.
    • Agencies are instructed to limit access to federal buildings for WilmerHale employees if it threatens national security.
    • Hiring of WilmerHale employees by government agencies requires a waiver.
  • The order emphasizes compliance with applicable laws and does not create any new enforceable rights.
