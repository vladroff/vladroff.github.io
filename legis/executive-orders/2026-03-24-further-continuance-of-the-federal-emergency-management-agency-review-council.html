FURTHER CONTINUANCE OF THE FEDERAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY REVIEW COUNCIL ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Tuesday, March 24, 2026

Headline: FEMA Review Council extended, oversight shifted to Homeland Security.

Why it matters: Keeps the FEMA Review Council operating for a set period and changes who oversees its activities, potentially impacting federal emergency management review.

What the order does:

  • Extends the FEMA Review Council’s term until its report is delivered or May 29, 2026, whichever comes first.
  • Transfers presidential oversight functions for the Council to the Secretary of Homeland Security.
  • Replaces earlier related executive order provisions with this order’s terms.
  • Specifies that DHS pays the publication costs of this order.

Who it affects:

  • Members of the FEMA Review Council.
  • Department of Homeland Security leadership and staff.
  • Federal agencies involved in FEMA oversight.

Practical impact on a regular person:

  • No immediate effect unless you interact with FEMA policies shaped by the Council’s recommendations.
  • Maintains federal review of emergency management for a longer period.

Context: Limits presidential discretion by assigning Council oversight to the Homeland Security Secretary per existing regulations.

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/03/further-continuance-of-the-federal-emergency-management-agency-review-council/
