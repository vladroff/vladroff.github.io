LAUNCHING THE GENESIS MISSION ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Monday, November 24, 2025

Headline: New “Genesis Mission” unites U.S. AI resources for breakthroughs in science and security.

Why it matters: This order launches the largest coordinated federal effort to use AI and national data for solving critical scientific, energy, and security challenges, aiming to secure America’s technological and strategic edge.

What the order does:

  • Establishes the Genesis Mission to drive national AI-powered scientific discovery.
  • Creates the American Science and Security Platform to unify high-performance computing, datasets, and AI modeling.
  • Directs the Department of Energy to integrate resources, ensure security, and identify key scientific challenges.
  • Requires regular identification and updating of priority national science and technology challenges.
  • Mandates interagency coordination and programs for fellowships, partnerships, and workforce training in AI-enabled research.
  • Sets requirements for annual reporting on progress, outcomes, and partnership results.

Who it affects:

  • Department of Energy and its national laboratories.
  • Federal agencies engaged in scientific research and AI.
  • Academic and private-sector research partners.
  • Researchers, students, and the scientific workforce.

Practical impact on a regular person:

  • Accelerates breakthroughs in energy, health, manufacturing, and tech—potentially improving everyday life.
  • Expands job, training, and fellowship opportunities in science and AI fields.

Context: The Genesis Mission is positioned as a Manhattan Project-scale, all-of-government initiative to ensure U.S. global leadership in AI and scientific innovation.

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/11/launching-the-genesis-mission/
