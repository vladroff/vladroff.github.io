Immediate Expansion of American Timber Production ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Saturday, March 1, 2025

Summary of Timber Production Executive Order ​

Section 1: Purpose ​

  • Timber production is essential for:
    • Construction
    • Energy production
    • Forest management and wildfire risk reduction, which can save lives
  • The U.S. has sufficient timber resources, but:
    • Federal policies hinder full utilization
    • Reliance on foreign lumber poses economic risks and job losses
  • Aims to reverse these policies to enhance domestic timber production and secure economic stability.

Section 2: Directives to the Secretaries ​

  • Within 30 days:

    • Issue updated guidance for increased timber production and forest management.
    • Submit legislative proposals to improve timber production capabilities.

  • Within 60 days:

    • Develop a strategy under the Endangered Species Act (ESA) to expedite forestry project approvals.

  • Within 90 days:

    • Submit a plan setting targets for annual timber sales from federal lands for the next 4 years.

  • Within 120 days:

    • Complete the Whitebark Pine Rangewide Programmatic Consultation under the ESA.

  • Within 180 days:

    • Consider adopting categorical exclusions to streamline processes for timber production and related activities.

  • Within 280 days:

    • Consider establishing a new categorical exclusion for timber thinning and restore for salvage activities.

Section 3: Streamlined Permitting ​

  • Agencies must eliminate undue delays in permitting for timber production.
  • Revise or rescind regulations that impose burdens on timber production.

Section 4: Endangered Species Committee (ESC) ​

  • Utilize ESA regulations for consultations in emergencies to boost timber production.
  • ESC is to review submissions efficiently and resolve exemption application issues promptly.
  • Federal members should report on obstacles to timber production due to ESA and recommend improvements.

Section 5: General Provisions ​

  • The order does not affect the authority of executive departments or agencies.
  • It will be implemented per applicable laws and existing appropriations.
  • The order does not create enforceable rights against the U.S. or its entities.

Date of Order: March 1, 2025
