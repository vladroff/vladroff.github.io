Immediate Declassification of Materials Related to the Federal Bureau of Investigation’s Crossfire Hurricane Investigation ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Tuesday, March 25, 2025

  • Subject: Immediate declassification of materials related to the FBI’s Crossfire Hurricane investigation.

  • Authority: Action taken under the authority of the President of the United States.

  • Declassification Decision:

    • All materials mentioned in the Presidential Memorandum from January 19, 2021 are deemed no longer classified.
    • Materials proposed for redaction by the FBI on January 17, 2021, will remain classified.

  • Exclusions:

    • Declassification does not pertain to materials requiring protection under Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court orders.
    • Personally identifiable information and other legally protected materials are excluded from disclosure.

  • Public Access:

    • The Attorney General is instructed to make the declassified materials available to the public immediately, subject to the stated exceptions.

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/immediate-declassification-of-materials-related-to-the-federal-bureau-of-investigations-crossfire-hurricane-investigation/
