MEASURES TO END CASHLESS BAIL AND ENFORCE THE LAW IN THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Monday, August 25, 2025

  • The Executive Order addresses a crime emergency in Washington, D.C., which is affecting federal operations.
  • It identifies the District's cashless bail policies as contributing to the crime issues, allowing dangerous individuals to be repeatedly released.
  • The policy mandates that:
    • Federal law enforcement agencies work to keep arrestees in custody whenever possible, especially those posing a public safety threat.
    • The Attorney General will review and recommend updates to Metropolitan Police Department policies to prevent pretrial release of dangerous individuals.
  • The Attorney General will assess whether D.C. continues its cashless bail policy concerning serious offenses, including violent crimes and property crimes.
  • If D.C. upholds such policies, federal agencies may take actions to influence a change, potentially affecting funding and services.
  • The order clarifies that it does not diminish existing legal authorities or create enforceable rights against the U.S. or its entities.
  • Implementation of the order will align with existing laws and available appropriations.

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/08/measures-to-end-cashless-bail-and-enforce-the-law-in-the-district-of-columbia/
