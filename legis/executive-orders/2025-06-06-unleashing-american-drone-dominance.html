UNLEASHING AMERICAN DRONE DOMINANCE ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Friday, June 6, 2025

  • Overview of Drones:

    • Drones (UAS) enhance productivity and create high-skilled jobs.
    • They're transforming multiple industries, including logistics, agriculture, and public safety.
    • Emerging technologies like eVTOL aircraft are set to modernize aviation.

  • Integration into National Airspace:

    • The U.S. aims to safely commercialize drone technologies and integrate UAS into the National Airspace System.

  • Key Policies:

    • Accelerate integration through risk-based rulemaking.
    • Support domestic commercialization with regulatory clarity.
    • Strengthen the drone industrial base and promote exports.

  • Action Items:

    • BVLOS Operations:

      • FAA to propose rules for routine BVLOS operations within 30 days.
      • Safety metrics for BVLOS operations to be established within 30 days.
      • Identify regulatory barriers and propose solutions within 180 days.

    • AI Deployment:

      • Implement AI tools for UAS waiver applications to expedite processes within 120 days.

    • eVTOL Integration Pilot Program:

      • Establish eIPP to accelerate eVTOL deployment, including public request for proposals within 90 days.
      • Recommendations and evaluations of the pilot program to be submitted throughout its duration.

  • Domestic Manufacturing Prioritization:

    • Agencies to prioritize U.S. manufactured drones.
    • Creation of a Covered Foreign Entity List to identify supply chain risks.

  • Export Controls:

    • Review and amend regulations to expedite export of U.S.-manufactured civil UAS to non-adversaries.

  • Department of Defense (DoD) Initiatives:

    • Ensure procurement of U.S. manufactured drones for military use.
    • Streamline airspace access for UAS training within 90 days.

  • General Provisions:

    • The order does not alter existing legal authorities.
    • Implementation subject to applicable laws and available funds.

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/06/unleashing-american-drone-dominance/
