ESTABLISHING THE WHITE HOUSE TASK FORCE ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Friday, March 7, 2025

Summary of Executive Order for FIFA World Cup 2025 and 2026 ​

  • Authority granted by the President of the United States under the Constitution and laws.

  • Establishment of the White House Task Force on the FIFA World Cup 2026.

    Key Points: ​

    • The U.S. is a host nation for the FIFA World Cup 2026.
      • Significance:
        • Largest sporting event in history.
        • Coincides with the 250th anniversary of the U.S.
        • Opportunity to promote national pride, hospitality, economic growth, and tourism.
    • Task Force Leadership:
      • Chair: The President
      • Vice Chair: The Vice President
      • Executive Director: Designated by the Chair to manage daily operations.

    Membership of the Task Force: ​

    • Secretary of State
    • Secretary of the Treasury
    • Secretary of Defense
    • Attorney General
    • Secretary of Commerce
    • Secretary of Transportation
    • Secretary of Homeland Security
    • Assistant to the President for National Security Affairs
    • Various Deputy Chiefs of Staff
    • Director of the FBI
    • Heads of other agencies as designated by the Chair or Vice Chair.

  • Functions of the Task Force:

    • Coordinate with agencies for the planning, organization, and execution of events surrounding the FIFA Club World Cup 2025 and the FIFA World Cup 2026.
    • Agencies need to assist and provide necessary information.

  • Administrative Support:

    • Task Force will be housed in the Department of Homeland Security for funding and administrative help.

  • Reporting:

    • Agencies involved must report their planning activities to the Task Force by June 1, 2025.

  • Termination:

    • Task Force will end on December 31, 2026, unless extended by the President.

Legal Notes: ​

  • This order does not impair the authority of executive departments or agencies.
  • Implementation is subject to applicable laws and appropriation availability.
  • The order does not create enforceable rights against the U.S. or its entities.

Date of Order: March 7, 2025

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/establishing-the-white-house-task-force-on-the-fifa-world-cup-2026/
