MODERNIZING DEFENSE ACQUISITIONS AND SPURRING INNOVATION IN THE DEFENSE INDUSTRIAL BASE ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Wednesday, April 9, 2025

  • The President emphasizes the importance of the U.S. military's capabilities and the defense industrial base in maintaining military strength.
  • Acknowledges that the current defense acquisition system is outdated, lacking the necessary speed and flexibility needed for future military advantages.
  • The objective is to modernize defense procurement and revitalizing the industrial base for enhanced national security through:
    • Accelerated and streamlined defense acquisitions.
    • A revamped defense acquisition workforce promoting innovation and risk-taking.

Key Directives:

  • Within 60 Days:

    • Secretary of Defense to submit a reform plan for acquisition processes prioritizing:
      • Use of expedited authorities for acquisitions (e.g., commercial solutions and Other Transactions Authority).
      • Review to eliminate unnecessary tasks and centralize decision-making in the acquisition workforce.
      • Establishment of a Configuration Steering Board to manage acquisition program risks.

  • Review Acquisition Regulations:

    • Revise unnecessary internal guidance and regulations while promoting expedited processes.
    • Apply the "ten-for-one" rule for any new regulations.

  • Within 120 Days:

    • Develop a plan to reform and train the acquisition workforce focusing on:
      • New performance metrics incorporating commercial solutions.
      • Analysis of staffing needs for effective capability delivery.
      • Creation of field training teams for innovative acquisition practices.
      • Incentivization of the use of innovative acquisition authorities.

  • Comprehensive Reviews:

    • Within 90 Days: Complete a review of all major defense acquisition programs (MDAPs) to identify those not aligning with policy objectives, considering potential cancellations for underperforming programs.
    • Within 180 Days: Review the Joint Capabilities Integration and Development System to streamline and accelerate acquisition processes.

Definitions:

  • Clarifies key terms related to acquisition processes, such as:
    • Adaptive Acquisition Framework and acquisition program baselines.
    • Commercial solutions and innovative acquisition authorities.

Implementation Notes:

  • The order does not affect established executive authorities or OMB functions.
  • Actions should be consistent with applicable law and contingent on appropriations.
  • No new enforceable rights are created through this order.
