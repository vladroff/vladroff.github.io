ADVANCING REGENERATIVE AGRICULTURE AND STRENGTHENING AMERICAN FARM RESILIENCE ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Thursday, June 25, 2026

Headline: Executive order ramps up support for regenerative agriculture and safer food supply.

Why it matters: The order aims to address childhood chronic diseases by improving food quality and safety, supporting American farmers with modern, sustainable practices, and protecting public health from harmful chemical exposures.

What the order does:

  • Increases federal investment in regenerative agriculture research, education, and modernization.
  • Prioritizes EPA registration of safer, alternative agricultural chemicals.
  • Expands funding and reach of the Regenerative Pilot Program.
  • Launches a prize challenge to spur research on cumulative chemical exposures and health impacts.
  • Promotes development of new, less hazardous crop protection technologies.
  • Mandates risk assessments and updated labeling for certain chemical uses in agriculture.

Who it affects:

  • Farmers and ranchers adopting regenerative and precision agriculture.
  • Agricultural supply companies and researchers working on safer farming inputs.
  • Federal agencies (USDA, HHS, EPA).
  • Consumers concerned about food safety and affordability.

Practical impact on a regular person:

  • Increased likelihood of healthier, safer, and more affordable food options.
  • Potential for reduced exposure to harmful chemicals in the food supply.

Context: The order follows a $1 billion federal investment and builds on the MAHA Commission’s mission to combat chronic disease by transforming agriculture.
