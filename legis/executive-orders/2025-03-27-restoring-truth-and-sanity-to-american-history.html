RESTORING TRUTH AND SANITY TO AMERICAN HISTORY ​

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/restoring-truth-and-sanity-to-american-history/

Published: 2025-03-27T18:58:09-04:00

Summary of Executive Order ​

Section 1: Purpose and Policy ​

  • Acknowledges a movement to distort American history by replacing objective facts with ideologically driven narratives.
  • Critiques prior administration's support of divisive race-centered ideologies affecting historical sites and institutions.
  • States the goal of restoring Federal historical sites to reflect achievements of liberty and progress, promoting unity rather than division.
  • Aims to revitalize the Smithsonian Institution as a beacon of American greatness.

Section 2: Saving Our Smithsonian ​

  • Vice President will collaborate to remove improper ideology from Smithsonian properties and recommend further actions.
  • Future appropriations to the Smithsonian will:
    • Ban funding for exhibits that undermine American values or promote divisive ideologies.
    • Ensure recognition of women in the American Women’s History Museum without acknowledgment of men as women.
  • Other relevant officials will take appropriate measures to support this order.
  • Vice President will seek citizen appointments to the Smithsonian Board of Regents aligned with these policies.

Section 3: Restoring Independence Hall ​

  • Funding will be allocated for infrastructure improvements at Independence National Historical Park, aiming for completion by July 4, 2026.

Section 4: Restoring Truth in American History ​

  • Secretary of the Interior will:
    • Assess changes to public monuments since January 1, 2020.
    • Reinstate original monuments or similar properties aligned with legal standards.
    • Ensure that all public monuments focus on positive historical narratives rather than disparaging content.

Section 5: General Provisions ​

  • Clarifies that the order does not affect existing authority of executive departments or the Office of Management and Budget.
  • Implementation is subject to applicable laws and budget availability.
  • The order does not grant any enforceable rights or benefits.
