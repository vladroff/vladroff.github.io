REFORMING NUCLEAR REACTOR TESTING AT THE DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY ​

Listen to the summary:

Published on Friday, May 23, 2025

  • Purpose: The Executive Order aims to revitalize the U.S. nuclear energy sector and promote the development of advanced nuclear technologies.

  • Background:

    • The U.S. has been a leader in civilian nuclear power development.
    • Commercial deployment of new nuclear technologies has stagnated since the 1970s due to regulatory challenges.

  • Significance of Nuclear Energy:

    • Nuclear energy is essential for a reliable and diverse energy supply supporting industries such as advanced technology, manufacturing, agriculture, and national security.
    • Advanced reactor designs have potential applications across various sectors including healthcare, petrochemicals, and hydrogen production.

  • Goals:

    • To foster innovation in nuclear technologies and encourage their domestic production.
    • To bring at least three new advanced reactors online by July 4, 2026, under a pilot program.

  • Definitions:

    • Advanced Reactor: Defined as per 42 U.S.C. 16271(b)(1).
    • Department: Refers to the Department of Energy.
    • Qualified Test Reactor: A reactor that can feasibly become operational within two years of a complete application.

  • Implementation Steps:

    • Guidance & Regulations:

      • The Secretary of Energy to issue guidance on qualified test reactors within 60 days.
      • Revise procedures to expedite review and approval of advanced reactors within 90 days.

    • Team Formation:

      • Establish a team to assist applicants in the application process for qualified test reactors.

    • Pilot Program:

      • Launch a pilot program to approve at least three reactors outside the National Laboratories.

    • Environmental Compliance:

      • Reform compliance with NEPA by June 30, 2025, in consultation with the Council on Environmental Quality.

  • Authority Clarification:

    • The order does not affect existing authorities of executive agencies or the Office of Management and Budget.
    • Implementation is subject to applicable laws and appropriations.

  • Funding: The Department of Energy will provide funding for the order's publication.
