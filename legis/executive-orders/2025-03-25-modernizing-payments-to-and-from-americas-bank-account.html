Modernizing Payments To and From America’s Bank Account ​

Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/modernizing-payments-to-and-from-americas-bank-account/

Published: 2025-03-25T15:57:57-04:00

  • Objective: Transition to electronic payments for all Federal disbursements and receipts to increase efficiency, reduce costs, and enhance security.

  • Key Actions:

    • Effective September 30, 2025, cease issuing paper checks for Federal disbursements, including benefits, vendor payments, and tax refunds, with some exceptions.
    • Mandate all federal agencies to comply by transitioning to electronic funds transfer (EFT) methods such as direct deposit and digital payment options.
    • Require that all payments to the Federal Government be processed electronically, barring specified exceptions.

  • Exception Policy:

    • The Secretary of the Treasury may grant exceptions for:
      • Individuals lacking access to banking services.
      • Emergency payments causing undue hardship.
      • National security or law enforcement needs.
      • Other circumstances deemed appropriate.

  • Implementation & Support:

    • Development of a public awareness campaign to assist Federal payment recipients with the transition to electronic payments.
    • Collaboration with financial institutions to improve access for unbanked and underbanked populations.
    • Protection of classified and personally identifiable information throughout the process.

  • Compliance & Reporting:

    • Agency heads must submit a compliance plan within 90 days.
    • The Secretary of the Treasury to report implementation progress within 180 days.

  • Legal Considerations:

    • The order does not affect existing legal authorities or create enforceable rights against the U.S. or its agencies.
